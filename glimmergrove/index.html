About the game

Glimmer Grove is a cozy idle garden game set in a quiet moonlit clearing. Tend glowing plants with Pip, your little lantern spirit, and watch the grove keep growing even while you are away. It is a relaxing game for slow evenings: tap when you feel like it, wander off when you do not.

Every plant, spirit and landmark is earned by playing. Glimmer Grove plays offline and has no timers that punish you. Pick it up for one minute or one hour.

The game has Moonlit Night and Daybreak themes, soft ambient music, accessibility options and a built-in Sanctuary & Item Guide (tap ? GUIDE any time). Google Play Games sign-in is optional, for achievements and cloud backup.

The game shows a small banner and occasional short video breaks. Bonus videos are always your choice, and a one-time No Ads purchase removes banners and automatic video breaks. It contains ads and optional in-app purchases.

How to play

  1. 1

    Harvest Dew

    Tap Pip or the garden to harvest Dew.

  2. 2

    Grow your garden

    Spend Dew on new plants and upgrades in the Garden Shop. Buy x1, x10 or MAX at a time.

  3. 3

    Build a Firefly Rhythm

    Tap plants and fireflies in quick succession to chain x1, x2, then a glowing Starlight Surge.

  4. 4

    Come back later

    The grove keeps collecting Dew in your Dew Jars and the Moon Well while you are away.

  5. 5

    Play a Night

    Gather the Dew target before dawn, earn up to three stars and light the lanterns along the Lantern Path.

  6. 6

    Plant the Seed Tree

    When you want a new season, plant the Seed Tree for permanent growth bonuses.
