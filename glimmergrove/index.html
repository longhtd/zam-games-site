About the game

Glimmer Grove is a cozy idle garden set in a moonlit forest of fireflies and glowing mushrooms. Meet Pip, a little leaf sprite who waters, harvests and cheers you on.

Plant, grow and restore a magical grove. Your plants fill with sparkling dust, and the Moon Well keeps collecting while you are away.

Befriend garden spirits, light up landmarks and begin a New Season to grow an even brighter grove.

How to play

  1. 1

    Visit the garden

    Tap Play to meet Pip in the glowing grove.

  2. 2

    Plant and harvest

    Buy plants with Glimmer Dust. They fill Dew Jars, and a tap collects the dust.

  3. 3

    Come back later

    While you are away the Moon Well keeps collecting. Collect it when you return.

  4. 4

    Restore landmarks

    Spend dust to bring the Lantern Path, Stone Bridge and Lake Shrine back to life.

  5. 5

    Befriend spirits

    Spirits join when you grow their favourite plants. Each has a special skill.

  6. 6

    Fill orders and quests

    Pick from three orders at a time, finish daily quests and level up your journal.

  7. 7

    Begin a New Season

    At 50K lifetime dust, restart for Seeds of Light and spend them in the Seed Tree.
