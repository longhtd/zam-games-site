About the game

Step inside a warm little cabin where every word you find helps the lodge glow. Word Lodge is a cozy word puzzle: swipe the letters on the wheel, fill the crossword grid, and wander through a storybook countryside at your own pace.

Travel 35 hand-painted worlds across 7 biomes, from the Golden Meadow to a starfall grove. Collect friendly companions, come back each day for a fresh Daily Puzzle and gift, and watch the lodge change from sunny afternoon to starry night.

It is built for calm: no timers, no pressure, and rewarded videos only when you choose to watch one.

How to play

  1. 1

    Swipe the letters

    Slide your finger across the letter wheel to spell a word, then lift to submit.

  2. 2

    Fill the crossword

    Valid words lock into the grid. Words cross and share letters, so each answer helps the next.

  3. 3

    Collect bonus words

    Words that are not in the grid still count as bonus words and pay coins.

  4. 4

    Tap the lightbulb

    A hint reveals a letter. Hints refill over time and can be topped up, and you can watch a video for one.

  5. 5

    Explore the worlds

    Open the map to travel through 35 worlds and earn stars and coins.

  6. 6

    Read your Word Journal

    Browse solved words by world and level, replay a level and mark words as learned.
