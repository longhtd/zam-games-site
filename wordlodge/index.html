About the game

Word Lodge is a cozy word connect game for anyone who loves a calm word puzzle. Swipe the letters on the wheel to spell words, fill the crossword grid and wander through 35 illustrated worlds at your own pace.

No time limits. No pressure. Play offline anywhere: on the sofa, on the train or by the fire. A relaxing break, and a word puzzle that quietly builds real English vocabulary as you go.

Word Lodge is free to play and contains ads and optional in-app purchases (coins, hint packs, ad removal and support packs). Rewarded videos are always optional, and support packs never unlock gameplay.

How to play

  1. 1

    Swipe the letters

    Slide your finger across the letter wheel to spell a word, then lift to submit. Shuffle the wheel whenever you like.

  2. 2

    Fill the crossword

    Valid words drop into the grid. Words cross and share letters, so each answer helps the next.

  3. 3

    Collect bonus words

    Extra valid words that are not in the grid still count and earn bonus coins.

  4. 4

    Chain a Word Streak

    Find 3 words in a row to reveal a hidden letter and win bonus coins.

  5. 5

    Stuck? Ask for help

    Tap the lightbulb or the Vowel Compass for a hint, or shuffle the wheel. Watching a video for a hint is always optional.

  6. 6

    Learn as you play

    Tap a found word to read its definition (27,000+ definitions), and browse your Word Journal by world and level.
