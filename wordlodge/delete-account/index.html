Data deletion

Word Lodge · Last updated: 2026-10-03

Word Lodge does not have user accounts of its own, and we do not store personal data about you on our own servers. Your game progress is stored on your device and, only if you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, also as a Saved Games backup in your Google account.

Delete your game data

  1. Open your Android Settings > Apps > Word Lodge.
  2. Choose Storage > Clear data (or Uninstall the app).

This permanently removes your saved progress from the device.

Google Play Games saved data

If you used Google Play Games sign-in, your saved game, achievements and leaderboard scores are held by Google. To delete them, open the Google Play Games app > Settings > Delete Play Games account and data, or manage them at myaccount.google.com. Signing out inside the game stops the game using them.

Advertising data

Ad-related data is held by the ad network, not by us. You can reset your advertising ID or delete ad activity from your Google account at myadcenter.google.com.

Questions or requests

If you want to ask us to delete any information we may hold, email support@zamverse.com.
