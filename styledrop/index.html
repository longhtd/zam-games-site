About the game

Style Drop is a dress up game where your memory runs the fashion show. Watch the model walk the runway, memorize every piece, then rebuild the outfit from the tray before the timer ends. Part fashion memory game, part dress up game, with quick rounds and no internet needed.

Beat the timer, match the pieces and earn up to 5 stars and coins. Spend coins in the Boutique, then style 12 themed briefs in the dress up room, from Coffee Run to Red Carpet.

Every runway level and every wardrobe piece works offline, and every piece can be earned by playing. Bonus videos are always optional, and an optional one-time Ad-Free upgrade is available. The game contains ads and optional in-app purchases.

How to play

  1. 1

    Watch the runway

    The model walks out in a look. Memorize every piece.

  2. 2

    Rebuild from the tray

    When the lights drop, rebuild the outfit from the tray. Decoys are mixed in.

  3. 3

    Beat the timer

    Match the pieces to earn up to 5 stars and coins.

  4. 4

    Spend coins in the Boutique

    Grow your wardrobe with 41 pieces for Top, Bottom, Shoes, Hair and Accessory.

  5. 5

    Style a themed brief

    Dress for 12 briefs in the dress up room and compare your look with the target, piece by piece.

  6. 6

    Come back daily

    Try the Daily Look, follow the Quest Board and claim the Daily Boutique Jackpot.
