About the game

Burrow Buddy is a cozy virtual pet game about a little woodland friend and the underground home you build together. Adopt a bunny, a hedgehog or a fox kit, feed and cook, wash and tuck in, then dig deeper to find new rooms. Calm, cute and made for short, happy sessions.

Care for your buddy with feeding, bath time and bedtime, grow ingredients in the garden, and dress them up with hats, scarves, glasses and fur tints with a live try-on. Check the 3-lane Living Quest Board each day and tap [? Guide] for an illustrated Care & Item Guide.

Burrow Buddy never rushes you: your burrow is saved on your device and plays offline. Purchases and external links ask a grown-up math question first. Bonus videos are always optional, and an optional one-time Ad-Free purchase removes banner and between-round ads. It contains ads and optional in-app purchases.

How to play

  1. 1

    Pick your buddy

    Pick a Rabbit, Hedgehog or Fox Kit, then tap to pet, feed, wash and tuck in your buddy.

  2. 2

    Keep the care meters full

    Keep the four care meters topped up to earn bonus coins on every care action.

  3. 3

    Play and dig

    Play mini-games and Dig Deep expeditions to earn coins.

  4. 4

    Make the burrow a home

    Spend coins on food, furniture and outfits to make the burrow a nicer home.

  5. 5

    Check in each day

    Visit the Living Quest Board every day, and tap [? Guide] to learn what any item does.
