About the game

Burrow Buddy is a gentle pet-care game. Choose a playful Rabbit, a shy Hedgehog or a sleepy Fox Kit and watch them react to every snack, bath and bedtime.

Keep Food, Energy, Clean and Fun topped up, then grow your burrow into a kitchen, bathroom, bedroom and garden.

Take a break with five quick mini-games and collect medals along the way.

How to play

  1. 1

    Choose your buddy

    Pick the animal who will live in your burrow. You can adopt others later.

  2. 2

    Watch the four meters

    Food, Energy, Clean and Fun slowly go down. Keep them full.

  3. 3

    Feed, wash and tuck in

    Drag food onto your buddy, scrub them clean and put them to bed.

  4. 4

    Grant wishes

    Thought bubbles show what your buddy wants. Wishes pay coins and XP.

  5. 5

    Level up and unlock rooms

    Pet levels 1 to 20 open the Kitchen, Bathroom, Bedroom and Garden.

  6. 6

    Play mini-games

    Earn bronze, silver and gold medals and coins.

  7. 7

    Finish daily goals

    Complete goals and keep a seven-day streak for bonus gems.
