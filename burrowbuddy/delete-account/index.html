Data deletion

Burrow Buddy · Last updated: 2026-10-17

Burrow Buddy does not have user accounts of its own, and we do not store personal data about you on our own servers. Your game progress is stored on your device.

Delete your game data

  1. Open your Android Settings > Apps > Burrow Buddy.
  2. Choose Storage > Clear data (or Uninstall the app).

This permanently removes your saved progress from the device.

Google Play Games data

If you signed in with Google Play Games, that data is held by Google. You can sign out in the app and delete your Play Games data from the Google Play Games app.

Advertising data

Ad-related data is held by the ad network, not by us. You can reset your advertising ID or delete ad activity from your Google account at myadcenter.google.com.

Questions or requests

If you want to ask us to delete any information we may hold, email support@zamverse.com.
