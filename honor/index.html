How it works

  1. 1

    Play a game

    Pick a Zamverse game. Every game is free to play, and more are coming to Google Play soon.

  2. 2

    Choose Support

    Support is an optional in-app purchase through Google Play. You can enter a name or stay anonymous. It never unlocks gameplay.

  3. 3

    See your name here

    After Google Play confirms the purchase, you appear on this page and on the wall inside the apps.

Support is not a charitable donation. Names are chosen by supporters and checked by filters. To report a name or ask for removal, email support@zamverse.com.
