About the game

Pixel Picnic is a cozy colour-sorting puzzle on a picnic blanket. Hungry swarms of ants and other bugs eat a pixel picture, one colour at a time.

Slots are limited, so plan the order. Every level is checked by a solver, so it can always be finished.

Spend your stars to dress up Picnic Park and fill the Creature Album with rare bug colours.

How to play

  1. 1

    Look at the picture

    A pixel picture sits on the blanket, hidden under layers.

  2. 2

    Tap a swarm

    Send a swarm to a slot. It eats every uncovered cell of its colour.

  3. 3

    Mind your slots

    Slots are limited. A swarm with nothing to eat holds its place, so plan the order.

  4. 4

    Learn new bugs

    Beetles need two slots, bees carry a key, and moths hide their colour.

  5. 5

    Use boosters if stuck

    Blanket, Lantern, Lane Swap, Net and Rain Shower can all help.

  6. 6

    Earn stars

    Stars reward skill. Spend them to dress up Picnic Park.

  7. 7

    Come back daily

    Daily Picnic, quests, the weekly race and the monthly pass.
