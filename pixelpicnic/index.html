About the game

Spread out the gingham blanket and welcome the ants! Pixel Picnic is a relaxing pixel puzzle where hungry critters march over a layered pixel art picture and eat it one color at a time.

Pick the right swarm at the right moment, keep your waiting slots from jamming and uncover what hides underneath. Nobody is racing the clock, so you can think at your own pace.

Collect critter variants in the Album, renovate the Picnic Park and turn your own photos into pixel puzzles with Photo Mode.

How to play

  1. 1

    Look at the picture

    A pixel picture sits on the picnic blanket. See which colors are uncovered on top.

  2. 2

    Tap a swarm

    Send a swarm at the bottom into a waiting slot. It eats every uncovered pixel of its color.

  3. 3

    Mind your slots

    Keep your waiting slots from jamming, and clear the top layer to expose the colors underneath.

  4. 4

    Meet new critters

    Beetles, bees, moths, snails, butterflies and fireflies add new twists.

  5. 5

    Use boosters if stuck

    Picnic Blanket, Lantern, Lane Swap, Net, Rain Shower and Rainbow Basket can all help.

  6. 6

    Tap Guide

    Get the rules in plain words and a live legend of every item on the board.

  7. 7

    Come back daily

    A Daily Picnic, a weekly Picnic Race, the Album and the Picnic Park.
