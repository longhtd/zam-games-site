Privacy Policy

Pixel Picnic · Last updated: 2026-10-17

This policy explains what information Pixel Picnic (“the app”), published by Zamverse (“we”, “us”), handles when you play. We built the game to collect as little as possible. Most of what you do never leaves your device.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, coins, unlocked scenery, hints and settings such as sound and music) is saved in a file on your device. We do not run a game server and we do not upload this file to one. Uninstalling the app removes the local copy.

Google Play Games Services (optional)

  • Signing in with Google Play Games is optional and starts only when you tap the sign-in button. The app works fully without it.
  • If you sign in, Google shares with the app your Play Games player ID, profile name and profile icon, which the app shows as your name and avatar.
  • Saved Games: a backup of your game progress (levels, coins, unlocked items, settings) is stored in your Google account by Google so you can restore it on a new phone. It is held by Google, not by us.
  • Achievements and leaderboard scores are sent to Google Play Games and may be visible to other players according to your Play Games settings.
  • This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can sign out in the app's settings, and delete your Play Games data from the Google Play Games app.

Photo Mode

Photo Mode lets you pick a picture from your phone and turn it into a pixel puzzle. The app uses the Android system photo picker, so it needs no storage permission and only receives the one picture you choose. The picture is turned into pixel art on your device and is not uploaded to us. A puzzle code you share contains only the small pixel puzzle (colors and layout), not your original photo.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account with us.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

Pixel Picnic shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the ad network may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (such as device model, operating system version and app version) and approximate location derived from the IP address. This data is handled by the ad network under its own policy, not by us: How Google uses data in ads and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent message. You can change your choice later from the game's settings when that option is shown. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, wording varies by device).

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play (Google Play Billing). We never see or store your payment card or billing details. The app asks Google Play which items you have bought (your purchase history for this app) so it can deliver them and restore them if you reinstall or change phone. We receive only confirmation that a purchase was completed so the item can be delivered to you.

Supporters and the Wall of Honor

Pixel Picnic may offer optional “Support” purchases. If you make one, you can choose to be recognised on the Wall of Honor. The Wall is public: it is shown on the Wall of Honor page at games.zamverse.com and inside Zamverse games that have a Wall, including games other than Pixel Picnic. For this feature, information is sent to a service operated by Zamverse.

  • What is sent. The display name you choose (or an anonymous setting), and the Google Play purchase token and product ID of the purchase. The service uses the token and product ID only to verify the purchase with Google Play. We do not receive and do not publish your email address or your real name.
  • Where it goes. To a service operated by Zamverse. The Wall is a public list that anyone can read, on games.zamverse.com, in the apps and as a public data file. For each supporter it contains only the chosen name (or “anonymous”), the game supported, the support level and total support amount in US dollars, the date, and a random code that identifies the entry and cannot be traced back to you.
  • Opening the Wall page. When you open the Wall of Honor page, your browser requests the public list from the Zamverse service (hosted on Cloudflare). Like any web request, this is visible to that service and its hosting provider. We set no cookies on the page and do not use the request to identify you.
  • Your name is shown only if you choose it. Anonymous is the default. A name appears only after you choose to enter one and Google Play has confirmed the purchase. If you stay anonymous, the Wall can still list an entry shown as “Anonymous supporter” with the game, support level and date, but no name.
  • How long we keep it. Your entry stays on the Wall, and the verification record is kept, for as long as needed to run the Wall, avoid duplicates and handle refunds and removal requests, unless you ask us to remove it sooner. If a purchase is refunded, your entry is removed.
  • How to remove it. Email support@zamverse.com with the name of the app and the name shown on the Wall, and we will remove or anonymise it. The Terms of Use explain the rules for supporter names.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app may remind you to come back and play (for example a daily puzzle or a streak reminder). These are local notifications: they are scheduled and shown by your device, not sent from a server, and no personal data is sent to us to create them. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your device settings at any time.

Children

Pixel Picnic is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at support@zamverse.com and we will take steps to delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete local game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. See Data deletion.
  • Manage ad personalisation in your device's Google settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we do not hold personal data about you on our own servers, there is usually nothing to retrieve, but you can ask us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your data (for example by adding a new service), we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before the change takes effect.
