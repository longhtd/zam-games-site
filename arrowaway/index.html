About the game

Arrow Away is a clean, satisfying logic puzzle. Every arrow waits to fly off the board if you tap them in the right order.

Tap an arrow and it glides out, but only if its path is clear. Tap a blocked one and you lose a heart, so read the board and plan.

Every world teaches one new idea, from stone walls and colour gates to spinning arrows.

How to play

  1. 1

    Tap an arrow

    It slides off the board in the direction it points.

  2. 2

    Keep the path clear

    An arrow only moves if nothing blocks it.

  3. 3

    Mind your hearts

    A blocked tap costs a heart. Finish with no mistakes for three stars.

  4. 4

    Learn the new pieces

    Walls, bends, colour gates, spinners and bombs arrive one world at a time.

  5. 5

    Use boosters when stuck

    Hint, Undo, Shield and Lift get you out of trouble.

  6. 6

    Collect stars and chests

    Every 30 stars opens a chest of coins, boosters and skin shards.

  7. 7

    Play the Daily Board

    Build a streak and protect it with a Streak Freeze.
