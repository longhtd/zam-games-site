About the game

Arrow Away is a calm arrow puzzle built on one simple wish: every arrow wants to escape the board. Tap an arrow and it slides straight out the way it points, but only if nothing blocks its path.

Read the lanes, find the right order and clear the whole maze. No rush, just clever thinking, whether you want a quick break or a long evening of brain-teasing boards. Tapping a blocked arrow costs a heart, and you get three hearts per board.

Every world adds a new rule, from switch gates and prism mirrors to conveyor belts, portals and bombs. A boss board closes each world and tests everything you have learned.

How to play

  1. 1

    Tap an arrow

    It slides out in the direction it points.

  2. 2

    Keep the path clear

    An arrow only leaves when its lane to the edge is clear.

  3. 3

    Mind your hearts

    A blocked tap costs a heart, and you get three per board. Fewer mistakes earn more stars.

  4. 4

    Switch on X-Ray

    Use the X-Ray button to see what is blocking each arrow.

  5. 5

    Learn the new pieces

    Switch gates, mirrors, conveyors, portals and bombs arrive one world at a time.

  6. 6

    Use boosters when stuck

    Undo, Shield, Lift and Hint get you out of trouble.

  7. 7

    Beat the boss board

    Each world ends with a boss board that tests everything you have learned.
