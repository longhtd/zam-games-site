About the game

Arrow Away is a calm arrow puzzle built on one simple wish: every arrow wants to escape the board. Tap an arrow and it slides straight out the way it points, but only if nothing blocks its path.

Read the lanes, find the right order and clear the whole maze. No rush, just clever thinking, whether you want a quick break or a long evening of brain-teasing boards. Tapping a blocked arrow costs a heart, and each board gives you 3 to 5 hearts.

New rules arrive every few levels, from switch gates and prism mirrors to conveyor belts, portals and bombs, and ten elemental arrows add a whole new layer. Boss boards close the big chapters and test everything you have learned, in waves.

How to play

  1. 1

    Tap an arrow

    It slides out in the direction it points.

  2. 2

    Keep the path clear

    An arrow only leaves when its lane to the edge is clear.

  3. 3

    Mind your hearts

    A blocked tap costs a heart, and each board gives you 3 to 5 hearts. Fewer mistakes earn more stars.

  4. 4

    Switch on X-Ray

    Use the X-Ray button to see what is blocking each arrow.

  5. 5

    Learn the new pieces

    Switch gates, mirrors, conveyors, portals and bombs arrive a few levels at a time, and element arrows start at level 25. Hold an element arrow to preview what a tap will do.

  6. 6

    Use boosters when stuck

    Undo, Shield, Lift and Hint get you out of trouble.

  7. 7

    Beat the boss boards

    Boss boards close the big chapters, the first at level 60, and test everything you have learned in several waves.
