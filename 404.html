Zamverse is an independent studio making simple, beautiful mobile games you can enjoy in a few minutes.

Zamverse is a small independent game studio from Vietnam. We build puzzle, casual and cozy games for phones, with one idea in mind: a game should feel like a deep breath, not a chore.

Each of our games starts from a single feeling, such as a quiet cabin, a moonlit garden or a sleepy cat, and we shape the rules, art and music around it.

We read every email. If something is unclear, broken or could simply be nicer, tell us and we will make it better.

What we care about

Easy to start

Every game teaches itself in seconds. No long tutorials and no complicated menus.

Made for short sessions

Play for two minutes in a queue or an hour on the sofa. Progress is saved on your device.

Warm and handmade

Hand-painted worlds and small characters you will want to come back to.

Respectful by design

Free to play, with optional purchases. We collect as little data as we can and explain it plainly in our privacy policy.
