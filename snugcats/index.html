About the game

Every cat needs a warm cushion. Snug Cats is a cozy logic puzzle where you place exactly one cat in every row, every column and every colored blanket, and keep them apart, even diagonally.

All 400 handmade levels are built so you can solve them by logic alone, from gentle 4x4 boards to deep 11x11 brain-teasers. Stuck? Smart Hints explain the logic in plain words instead of just giving the answer.

Finish a puzzle and the whole family curls up for a Nap Moment.

How to play

  1. 1

    Read the blankets

    The board is split into stitched, colored blanket regions.

  2. 2

    Place one cat in each

    Every row, every column and every blanket needs exactly one cat.

  3. 3

    Keep cats apart

    No two cats may touch, not even diagonally.

  4. 4

    Tap to cross, hold to place

    Tap a square to cross it out. Double-tap or hold to place a cat.

  5. 5

    Use Smart Hints

    Hints explain the logic in plain words. Paw View previews the exclusion zones.

  6. 6

    Learn in Nap School

    Short lessons from Luna teach the tricks, step by step.

  7. 7

    Settle in

    Solve the puzzle and the cats curl up for a Nap Moment.
